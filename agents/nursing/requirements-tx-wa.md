**Updated:** 2026-09-29  
**Role:** RN licenses in **Texas** and **Washington**  
**Renewal month (both):** **October**  
**Purpose:** Vet courses so they can count for **both** states when possible.

## State-specific CE (mandatory topics) — quick view

Targeted hours usually **count inside** the state total (not added on top), except noted.

### Texas RN (renew **October**, every **2 years**)

| Topic | Hours | When | Must be | Counts toward 20? | CareCEUs? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **General CNE** (area of practice) | **20** total / 2 yrs | Every renewal | TX BON–recognized provider (incl. other state BONs) | — | Yes (unlimited OK) |
| **Human trafficking prevention** | Per HHSC course (often 1–3) | Every renewal if direct patient care | **HHSC-approved only** | Yes | **No** — use [HHSC list](https://www.hhs.texas.gov/services/family-safety-resources/texas-human-trafficking-resource-center/health-care-practitioner-human-trafficking-training/human-trafficking-prevention-course-list) |
| **Nursing jurisprudence & ethics** | **2** | Every **3rd** 2-yr period (~every 6 yrs) | Must cover **Texas** NPA, BON rules §217.11, position statements, ethics, boundaries | Yes | **Unlikely** — need TX-specific course |
| **Older adult / geriatric** | **2** | Every 2-yr period **if** practice includes older adults | Any acceptable CNE on geriatric topics | Yes | Yes (e.g. Safely Caring for Seniors) |
| **Forensic evidence collection** | **≥2** | One-time if ER / forensic role | Acceptable CNE | Yes | Only if role applies |

## Texas (Board of Nursing)

**Sources:** [TX BON CNE](https://www.bon.texas.gov/education_continuing_education.asp.html), 22 TAC §216

| Item | Typical rule |
| --- | --- |
| Hours | **20 contact hours** CNE in area of practice per **2-year** licensing period **or** Board-approved national certification |
| Providers | Programs approved by a **Board-recognized credentialing agency/provider** (see BON list) |
| Licensing period | Tied to **birth month**; usually 2 years |
| Targeted topics | See table above |
| Records | Keep certificates; **CE upload in Texas Nurse Portal required for renewals starting Sep 1, 2026** (SB 912) |

**TX tip for dual use:** Prefer courses with clear **contact hours** and accreditation TX recognizes (e.g. ANCC and other BON-listed approvers).

## Washington (Board of Nursing)

**Sources:** [WA continuing competency](https://nursing.wa.gov/licensing/continuing-competency), WAC 246-840-220 / -222

| Item | Typical rule |
| --- | --- |
| CE hours | **8 hours** continuing nursing education **every year** (renewal period; birthday-based) |
| Of those 8 | At least **2 hours health equity** each renewal (timeline: by 2026 renewal for existing RNs, then every renewal) |
| Practice hours | **96 hours** active nursing practice per year (separate from CE) |
| One-time | **6-hour suicide prevention** training from a **DOH model-list** program (if not already done) |
| Formal CNE | Formal contact hours **not always required**, but nursing-related education that meets board rules is; **ANCC-accredited** courses are commonly used and easier to dual-count with TX |

**WA tip for dual use:** A TX-acceptable accredited CNE course on nursing practice often also satisfies WA’s 8-hour CE **if** hours and topic fit — still verify health equity / suicide rules separately.

---

## Dual-state strategy

1. Prefer **ANCC or other TX BON–recognized** accredited courses with stated **contact hours**.  
2. Map each certificate into **TX 20-hr / 2-yr bucket** and **WA 8-hr / 1-yr bucket**.  
3. Explicitly tag: Health Equity (WA), Jurisprudence/Ethics (TX), Trafficking (TX), Suicide (WA one-time), Geriatrics (TX if needed).  
4. Same course can often serve both **general** hour totals; mandatory topics are pickier.  
5. Rodney completes all tests; agent only vets and navigates.
