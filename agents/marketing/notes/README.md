### Messaging angles (from the book)

- Lead with **patience vs urgency** — up legs historically ~3 years, not days.
- **Halving ≠ peak:** next halving ~Apr 2028; historical peak lag ~17–18 months → ~fall 2029 (hold loosely).
- **Small sample** honesty (3–4 cycles) builds trust; don’t sell calendar certainty.
- Shrinking multiples / drawdowns = mature-market story, not “missed the only run.”
- Align offers with **staged taking of profits** and emotional regulation (Peak Performance Protocol framing in the PDF).
