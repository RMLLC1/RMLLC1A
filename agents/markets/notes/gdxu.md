## What the U.S. GDXU is

- **Not gold bullion** and **not** a plain gold-miners ETF like GDX.
- Seeks **three times** the **daily** performance of an index built from gold-mining ETFs (GDX / GDXJ weighted), **compounded daily**, minus fees.
- Designed for **short-term / tactical** use. Multi-day returns usually **do not** equal 3× the index over the same period (volatility decay / path dependency).
- As an **ETN**, you have **issuer credit risk** (Bank of Montreal / BMO Capital Markets family of notes) in addition to market risk. Maturity cited in materials around **June 2040**; issuer may have call features.

## Key risks

1. **3× daily leverage** — larger swings than UXRP’s 2× crypto product; losses can be severe in one session.  
2. **Daily reset** — choppy markets can grind value even if miners end “flat.”  
3. **Gold-miner equity risk** — tracks mining companies, not spot gold 1:1 (operational, equity-market, and gold-price sensitivity).  
4. **ETN credit / call / liquidity risk** — different from holding an ETF’s basket.  
5. **Fees** — investor fee often cited ~**0.95%** per annum (plus financing embedded in leveraged notes).  
6. **Extreme range** — third parties show very wide 52-week ranges (roughly mid‑$60s to ~$540 depending on vendor); treat as high-volatility.
