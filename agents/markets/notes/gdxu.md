## What it is

**GDXU** is the **MicroSectors™ Gold Miners 3X Leveraged ETN** — a U.S.-listed **exchange-traded note**, **not** an ETF, and **not** a spot gold or gold-miners ETF.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Issuer | **Bank of Montreal** (BMO); brand REX MicroSectors |
| Ticker | GDXU (NYSE Arca) |
| Structure | **ETN** — senior unsecured obligation of BMO (credit risk of the bank) |
| Objective | **3× the daily** performance of the **S-Network MicroSectors Gold Miners Index** (before fees/charges) |
| Inception / maturity | First trade **2020-12-02**; scheduled maturity **2040-06-29** (issuer may extend) |
| Investor fee | **0.95%** per annum, accrued daily |
| Financing | Daily financing charge also accrues (issuer: Prime Loan Rate + a financing spread; spreads can be changed per pricing docs) |
| Typical use (issuer framing) | Short-term / tactical daily trading tool; **not** a buy-and-hold product |

**Clarification:** Holding GDXU is **not** the same as owning gold bullion, GDX, or GDXJ. Returns target **daily 3×** index performance; multi-day results can diverge sharply from 3× of the miners’ multi-day move (compounding / volatility decay). As an **ETN**, value also depends on BMO’s creditworthiness, not just the index.

## Underlying exposure

- **Benchmark:** S-Network MicroSectors Gold Miners Index (total-return style index of two gold-miner ETFs).
- **Index mix (issuer disclosures; weights change):** roughly **~75% VanEck Gold Miners ETF (GDX)** and **~25% VanEck Junior Gold Miners ETF (GDXJ)** — i.e. equity exposure to gold mining companies, not physical gold.
- **Mechanism:** Leveraged long via the ETN’s daily reset payoff linked to that index (not a basket of miner stocks held in an ETF portfolio for the investor).
- **Not:** Spot gold, a 1× miners ETF, or unlevered GDX/GDXJ.

## Key risks

1. **Leverage (3× daily)** — Larger than UXRP’s 2×; single-day gains and losses are heavily amplified. A large adverse day can wipe out a large fraction of value; issuer docs warn indicative value at/below $0 can mean total loss.
2. **Daily reset / path dependency** — Holding beyond one day often does **not** equal 3× the index’s multi-day return; choppy markets can erode value even if miners end flat-to-up.
3. **Gold-miner equity risk** — Exposure is to **mining stocks** (operations, costs, jurisdictions, equity beta), which can diverge from the gold price itself.
4. **ETN credit / issuer risk** — Unsecured debt of Bank of Montreal; not an ownership claim on underlying assets; not FDIC-insured.
5. **Fees & financing drag** — ~0.95% investor fee plus daily financing; both compound against holders over time.
6. **Call / redemption / liquidity** — Issuer call rights, redemption fees, and bid-ask/volume matter; leveraged products can gap and trade away from indicative value in stress.
7. **Extreme drawdowns** — Third-party ranges show very wide 52-week swings; large simulated (or real) losses are plausible quickly.

## Cautious note vs UXRP (educational only)

Rodney already holds a **paper UXRP** position (~10% of the $100k paper book). Side-by-side for education:

| | **UXRP** (held) | **GDXU** (research only) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asset flavor | Crypto (XRP via futures) | Gold-miner **equities** (via index of GDX/GDXJ) |
| Leverage | **2×** daily | **3×** daily (higher) |
| Wrapper | Leveraged **ETF** | Leveraged **ETN** (issuer credit risk) |
| Structure risk | Futures / tracking | Credit of BMO + financing + daily reset |

Both are **daily-reset leveraged** products meant for short horizons. GDXU adds **higher leverage** and **ETN credit risk**; UXRP adds **crypto** volatility. Neither is a substitute for the other, and stacking both would concentrate **leverage + path-dependency** risk in the paper book.
