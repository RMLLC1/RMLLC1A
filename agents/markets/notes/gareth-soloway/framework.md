# Soloway playbook (distilled)

Source: public videos ~Aug–Sep 2026 on @GarethSolowayProTrader. Educational synthesis only.

## Core style

- **“No B.S. Just Charts.”** Technical structure first; fundamentals as confirmation.
- Heavy use of **multi-timeframe** charts (daily + weekly), **trend lines**, **parallel channels**, **double tops/bottoms**, **cup-and-handle**, **measured moves / scene of the crime** retraces after breakouts.
- **Macro cross-asset:** 10-year yield, oil, dollar, gold/silver, S&P, Bitcoin often analyzed **together** (divergences matter).
- Prefers **waiting for levels** over chasing; “nibble” language at supports.
- Distinguishes **swing trades** vs **physical / long-term holds** (esp. gold).
- Cycle work: long-horizon gold targets; depression/debt cycle commentary into 2029–2031 — treat as **scenario**, not certainty.

## Recurring causal map (his framing)

1. **Oil ↔ yields** usually move together; **divergence** (oil down, yields up) is a warning omen for stocks.
2. **Yields up → pressure on gold/silver** near-term; long-term gold bull intact via debt / debasement thesis.
3. **Fed path** (hike / hawkish) can spike dollar & yields; he still maps **technical rejection** levels on the 10-year.
4. **Bitcoin** judged by **line-in-the-sand** supports and whether bear-market structure is broken.
5. **Semis / AI / memory** — separate tactical book; wary of glut / mean reversion after euphoria.

## Trade hygiene he models

- Define invalidation (break of line / zone).
- Scale in at successive supports (“nibble”).
- Separate timeframe: short-term flush vs multi-year target (e.g. gold $13k thesis).
- Watch economic prints (JOLTS, confidence, ADP, NFP, CPI) as catalysts around technical levels.
