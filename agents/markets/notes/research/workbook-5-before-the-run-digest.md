## Core stance

- Sample is tiny: **4 Bitcoin cycles**, only **~3** mature enough to count. Treat patterns as **patience tools**, not calendar certainty.
- Urgency is the expensive emotion. Historically the **up leg lasts ~3 years** (~1,050–1,068 days) — feeling “three days late” is the wrong timescale.
- Plan must work if the peak is **2028**, **2029**, *or* if this cycle **looks nothing like** the last three. Selling in **sections on the way up** (price/behavior triggers) beats aiming at a date.

## Cycle legs (rule of thumb in the book)

- Down (ATH → cycle low): ~**364–383 days** average (~410 / 363 / 376).
- Up (low → next ATH): ~**1,050–1,068 days** (~1,068 / 1,061 / 1,050). Author’s thumb: ~**1,064 up / ~364 down**.

## Multiples and drawdowns shrinking

- Low→ATH multiples (rough): 569x → ~100–130x → 21.5x → **8.0x** (latest).
- Peak→trough drawdowns: 93.7% → 83.9% → 83.4% → **76.9%**.
- Implication: don’t silently assume another **100-bagger** on the whole book; size the story down; still take partial profits.

## Advice filters when Rodney asks about trades

1. Prefer **staged exits** (aligns with our paper scale-outs) over “wait for the cycle top.”
2. Challenge **FOMO / urgency** with duration math (~1,000+ day ups).
3. Separate **halving year** vs **historical peak lag**.
4. Stress-test any thesis against “cycle breaks / ETF-era regime change.”
5. Keep emotional regulation: written plan, present-moment rules, gains counted when realized.
